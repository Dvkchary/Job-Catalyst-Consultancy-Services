# JobCatalyst AI - Platform User Guide
## Document Version: 1.0.0-RC1 (Commercial Edition)

Welcome to **JobCatalyst AI**! This guide is designed to help job seekers, career transitioners, and recruiters maximize their success on our commercial career-acceleration platform.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Onboarding
When you first log in to JobCatalyst AI, complete these three setup steps:
1. **Upload your Base Resume**: Go to **My Resumes** and upload your current CV in PDF or DOCX format.
2. **Setup your Match Profile**: Navigate to your profile settings and input your target titles, salary expectations, preferred industries, and list of critical skills.
3. **Verify notifications**: Ensure email notifications or desktop alerts are authorized to receive real-time alerts about premium match openings.

---

## 2. Advanced AI Resume Optimization
The core of JobCatalyst AI's engine is our smart resume optimizer powered by the Gemini API.
* **ATS Keyword Density**: Upload a target job description alongside your resume. The engine scans for missing hard skills, certifications, and technical terminology.
* **STAR Impact Rewriting**: The engine automatically refines your existing bullet points to follow the **Situation, Task, Action, Result (STAR)** model, inserting measurable success metrics and action verbs.
* **Visual Formatting Checks**: Ensures margin constraints, standard fonts (Inter, Helvetica), and parsing rules are fully compliant with standard ATS scanners.

---

## 3. Real-time Applications Tracking
The **Track Pipelines** panel serves as your central job application Kanban board:
* Drag application cards across stages: **Wishlist**, **Applied**, **Interviewing**, **Offer**, and **Accepted**.
* Log specific dates, interviewer names, and email logs.
* Attach specific customized cover letters and tailored resumes directly to each application card.

---

## 4. Designing Automation Workflows
For premium and enterprise tiers, the **AI Workflows** console lets you build automated sequences:
1. **Define a Trigger**: Select what triggers your pipeline (e.g., when a draft resume is uploaded, or daily on a specific schedule).
2. **Sequence Action Steps**: Chain tasks together—such as running an ATS match score check, applying STAR impact rewriting, drafting custom cover letters, and sending an alert.
3. **Interactive Gates**: For high-trust actions like submitting applications, inject an **Approval Gate**. The pipeline will pause and alert you to review and click "Approve" before submitting.
